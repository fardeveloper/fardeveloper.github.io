I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Somewhere. My research focuses on the intersection of natural language processing, machine learning, and AI safety. I am particularly interested in developing methods that make large language models more interpretable and aligned with human values.

Before joining the University of Somewhere, I completed my Ph.D. at MIT under the supervision of Prof. Jordan Chen, and held postdoctoral positions at Stanford and Google Research.

Research Interests

My work spans several interconnected themes:

  • Mechanistic Interpretability — Understanding how transformers implement algorithms and store world knowledge through careful circuit analysis.
  • Alignment & Safety — Developing methods to ensure language models behave consistently with human intent, even in distribution-shifted settings.
  • Low-resource NLP — Building robust multilingual models for under-resourced languages using cross-lingual transfer.
  • Evaluation Methodology — Designing rigorous benchmarks that measure what we actually care about.
