Welcome

Dr. Elena
Vasquez

Assistant Professor of Computational Linguistics at the Institute for Language & Cognition, MIT. I study how humans and machines process language — from syntax to semantics, from psycholinguistics to large language models.

My research sits at the intersection of formal linguistics, cognitive science, and natural language processing. I'm particularly interested in how recursive structures emerge in grounded communication.
